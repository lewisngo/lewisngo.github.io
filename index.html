About

I'm currently a master's student in mathematics at the University of Bonn, working under the supervision of William Balderrama. My main interests lie in higher algebra and stable homotopy theory, especially its chromatic and equivariant aspects.

Previously, I completed my bachelor's degree at the Technical University of Munich under the supervision of Claudia Scheimbauer and Tashi Walde.
