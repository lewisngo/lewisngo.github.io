Master's thesis

Chromatic Splittings of Classifying Spaces

Manuscript in preparation

Show abstract Hide abstract

We give a complete classification of \(K(1)\)-local stable splittings of classifying spaces of finite groups at all primes, using their representation theory and ambidexterity of the \(K(1)\)-local category. At odd primes, the problem admits a completely algebraic description in terms of \(p\)-adic \(K\)-theory. All indecomposable building blocks arise from cyclic \(p\)-groups, with multiplicities governed by the permutation character of the power-map action on conjugacy classes of elements of \(p\)-power order. At the prime \(2\), rational representation theory yields additional dihedral, semidihedral, and generalized quaternion families, whose multiplicities are recovered from the actions of the Adams operations on \(K(1)^0(\mathrm{B}G)\) and \(\mathrm{KO}_2^3(\mathrm{B}G)\). We further determine the tensor products and homotopy groups of these summands, and extend the classification to \(E(1)\)-localization using the chromatic fracture square. Beyond height one, we obtain results on the \(K(n)\)-local splitting problem for classifying spaces of all cyclic \(p\)-groups at higher chromatic heights, using the action by the Morava stabilizer group.

Going forward, we plan to explore whether the prime-two classification admits a more conceptual description through united \(K\)-theory and investigate connections with classical integral stable splittings. In particular, we are interested in using the arithmetic fracture square to relate the height-one splittings to the categorical fixed points of the genuine \(\mathrm{KU}_G\)-local sphere. More broadly, we would like to explore such questions in the framework of global homotopy theory, investigate \(K(n)\)-local splittings of iterated classifying spaces \(\mathrm{B}^nG\) of finite abelian \(p\)-groups in connection with the higher cyclotomic Galois extensions of Carmeli-Schlank-Yanovski, and understand which features of the height-one picture persist at higher chromatic heights through HKR character theory.

Bachelor's thesis

Operadic Right Kan Extensions

Manuscript available upon request

Show abstract Hide abstract

We study colored operads over a symmetric monoidal ground category, together with modules and algebras over operads. We investigate restriction functors between categories of operadic algebras induced by morphisms of operads. We present a necessary and sufficient condition for such a restriction functor to admit a right adjoint. This condition is expressed in terms of a factorization property, requiring operations in the codomain operad to decompose essentially uniquely into operations of arity one, followed by operations in the domain operad. As an application, we show that the forgetful functor from cyclic operads to operads admits a right adjoint.
